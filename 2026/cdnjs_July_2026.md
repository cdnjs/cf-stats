Information provided directly by Cloudflare for the `cdnjs.cloudflare.com` domain. ⛅️

## Key highlights

- cdnjs served **nearly 230 billion requests** in July 2026. 🖥
- cdnjs used **a huge consumption of 5.42 PB of data** to serve these requests this month. 📤
- That's **just under 175 terabytes of data and 7.4 billion requests every day** (averaged). 🤯
- In July, **each request to cdnjs (on average) used only 23.63 KB of data**. 🔍
 
### Library highlights

- The top libraries overall (in the top 100 assets) this month were font-awesome, jquery,
  and angular.js.
  - Across the 27.76 billion requests for font-awesome,
    1,501.75 TB of bandwidth was consumed.
  - jquery received 14.98 billion requests and consumed 403.39 TB
    of bandwidth, with angular.js assets in the top 100 getting 10.39 billion requests and
    using 129.79 TB of bandwidth to be served.
  - webfont came in 4th this month with 4.96 billion requests, and
    slick-carousel in 5th with 4.70 billion.
- The top asset on the CDN this month was jquery.min.js from version 3.6.0 of
  jquery, reaching a total of 7.53 billion requests and using 202.31 TB
  of bandwidth to serve the file.
  - webfont (1.6.28/webfontloader.js) came in second with
    4.96 billion requests, followed by lodash.js
    (4.17.11/lodash.core.min.js) with 4.34 billion requests.

## Total number of requests

> The first important stat that we are given is the total number of requests sent to cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
> 
> Cloudflare provides this number to us at a 1% sample for the whole month, giving 2,296,041,295 at 1%.

When multiplied up to 100%, this results in cdnjs serving approximately 229,604,129,500 requests in July.

**Nearly 230 billion requests or around 7.4 billion requests every day of July**. 📈

## Total bandwidth usage

> Another great stat that Cloudflare has given us again is the bandwidth usage for the cdnjs.cloudflare.com domain.
> 
> This number, like total requests, is provided at a 1% sample for the month and in gigabytes: 54,245.34 GB.

When multiplied up to be 100%, this produces the estimate of 5,424,534.0 GB of bandwidth used for this month by
 cdnjs, or 5.42 PB.

**This gives cdnjs a huge bandwidth consumption of 5.42 petabytes of data for requests in July**. 🤯
