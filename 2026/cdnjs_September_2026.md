## Key highlights

- cdnjs served **over 254 billion requests** in September 2026. 🖥
- cdnjs used **a huge consumption of 6.04 PB of data** to serve these requests this month. 📤
- That works out to **over 201 TB of data and 8.5 billion requests each day** (averaged). 🤯
- In September, **each request to cdnjs used only 23.77 KB of data on average**. 🔍
 
### Library highlights

- The top libraries overall (in the top 100 assets) this month were font-awesome, jquery,
  and angular.js.
  - Across the 29.91 billion requests for font-awesome,
    1,633.56 TB of bandwidth was consumed.
  - jquery received 18.16 billion requests and consumed 496.01 TB
    of bandwidth, with angular.js assets in the top 100 getting 10.69 billion requests and
    using 134.64 TB of bandwidth to be served.
  - webfont came in 4th this month with 5.43 billion requests, and
    slick-carousel in 5th with 5.10 billion.
- The top asset on the CDN this month was jquery.min.js from version 3.6.0 of
  jquery, reaching a total of 9.79 billion requests and using 262.64 TB
  of bandwidth to serve the file.
  - webfont (1.6.28/webfontloader.js) came in second with
    5.43 billion requests, followed by lodash.js
    (4.17.11/lodash.core.min.js) with 4.97 billion requests.

## Total number of requests

> The first important stat that we are given is the total number of requests sent to cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
> 
> Cloudflare provides this number to us at a 1% sample for the whole month, giving 2,540,762,779 at 1%.

When multiplied up to 100%, this results in cdnjs serving approximately 254,076,277,900 requests in September.

**Over 254 billion requests or roughly 8.5 billion requests each day of September**. 📈

## Total bandwidth usage

> Another great stat that Cloudflare has given us again is the bandwidth usage for the cdnjs.cloudflare.com domain.
> 
> This number, like total requests, is provided at a 1% sample for the month and in gigabytes: 60,406.19 GB.

When multiplied up to be 100%, this produces the estimate of 6,040,619.0 GB of bandwidth used for this month by
 cdnjs, or 6.04 PB.

**This gives cdnjs a huge bandwidth consumption of 6.04 petabytes of data for requests in September**. 🤯
